# A field guide to shade

[Method](docs/method.md) · [Findings](docs/findings.md) · [Open](OPEN.md)

**Where you can sit down out of the sun, block by block, written by the people who sat there.** This is a
component bottle: a pile of plain files with a README for a door. Nobody built a website out of it, and you
are reading it anyway.

## What is in here

- `docs/method.md` — how a bench gets counted
- `docs/findings.md` — what was counted, as a table
- `CONSTITUTION.md` — what this guide will and will not do with what you tell it

> A guide like this is only as good as the last person who walked the block. If yours is newer, say so.

## Reading it without a renderer

Everything above degrades to what it already is. The strip under the title is a row of links. The front
matter is a table of the canonical files. That is the scheme working, not failing.
