# CONSTITUTION

This guide records where shade is. It does not record who told it.

1. A finding names a place and a time of day, never a person.
2. A finding may be corrected by anyone who was there more recently.
3. Nothing here is sold.

This is the text as it was when this bottle was shelved. A constitution's live text is whatever its own
address serves now; a copy you are handed is a memory of it.
