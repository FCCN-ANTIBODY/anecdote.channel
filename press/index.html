anecdote.channel

the press

One page that feels like one place. Under it, every view is a frame onto some other origin in the subdomain world — and two gestures: say a thing with an object attached, or read an object and put it down where bottles go.

  1. The Halt

    single attention

    You arrive at nothing, and it says so. One line at the bottom is the whole interface: a statement, an @place, or a ~pile. Exactly one thing is on purpose at a time.

  2. The Press

    bed · sheet · rack

    A printing press, left to right: where a statement is set, what is in front of you, and what is within reach. Three panes you swipe between on a phone.

  3. The Counter

    two people, present

    A phone held between two people and two verbs, each half the screen: hand over, or take. Reading is a sheet that opens over the counter and closes back to it.

  4. Posting mode

    over whatever is rendering

    The page is the place you are looking at, edge to edge. One button drops a posting surface over it, and what you say points at the version you were reading.
