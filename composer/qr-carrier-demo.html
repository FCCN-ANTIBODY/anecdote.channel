Carry a signed blob as a QR

The 99% carrier isn't a URL — it's deflated bytes with a signature inside. This signs a payload with your device's Ed25519 key, compresses it, and renders the raw octets as a byte-mode QR of whatever size the data needs (version auto-picks up to 40). Everything is on-device: WebCrypto signs, qr-encode.mjs draws, and a self-decode reads it back to prove the bytes survive.
