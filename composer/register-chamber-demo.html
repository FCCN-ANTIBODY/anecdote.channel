The consent chamber

GitHub's propose-then-a-human-merges, represented between two offline phones as an exchange of QR scans. Device A opens a registration; the ask travels as a byte-mode QR (deflated signed envelope, not a URL); Device B reads it off the code, decides, and sends a signed consent receipt back the same way. No network, no GitHub in the loop — and the verified pair replays onto the mirror as the ordinary _data/*.yml entry. Everything is composer/register-exchange.mjs over the repo's own QR carrier; each hand-off decodes the actual QR modules, so the bytes really make the trip.
